# Causal completion candidate, Issue #82

One request and one sample, N=1, full PHY/MAC/SDN/APP/ENV/OBS composition.
This is a changed-model candidate, not a frozen baseline or a production
generator change. Deterministic compilation: `python3 -B
evidence/instantiation/uav-service-completion-candidate/generate.py`.
`--check` reproduces every generated byte from the hash-checked historical XML.

The actual APP service_request sender assigns request ID 1 and resets the
service clock; demand/build/admission/grant do not reset it. B_ADMISSION keeps
its original loss, timeout, unsolicited-outcome and safety downgrade rules.
Only APP receipt of accepted or allowed degraded admission admits this ID.

The new ResultJob binds that admitted ID to a PHY A_SQ acquisition. The PHY
must first reach SensingQoSOk through its original measurement/report logic.
It then accepts c82_sense_start and spends exactly the historical D_sense=5
units measuring in JobMeasuring. This fixed acquisition duration is a new
explicit abstract assumption, not a physical measurement claim. An actual
matching c82_measurement event stores Pd, false alarm, missed detection,
accuracy and coverage from PHY at acquisition completion and resets sample
age. A nondeterministic acquisition-failure branch produces no sample, even
with healthy inputs. Unrelated PHY target/KPI notifications never produce a
job result. Stored quality cannot be overwritten by later global KPI changes.

The single result token enters the existing numerical queue through a
SharedLoad handshake. The original K=4 occupancy/overflow abstraction and
class thresholds are preserved. Its FIFO rank is pre-insertion occupancy+1.
Background arrivals enter behind it. Each eligible shared MAC service reduces
rank; only service of rank 1 dispatches this sample. The original optional
one-service-per-five-unit epoch, COMM/JOINT eligibility and absorbing K+1
overflow witness remain. A full insertion sets overflow and fails the job.
An absorbing overflow cannot dispatch the result. Background grants without
this token never advance it. The original PHY-command ACK protocol is retained
and is separate from result transmission; it does not establish delivery.

After dispatch, ResultJob records a transmission attempt. Within the existing
D_bus=1 abstract bound it may deliver through a binary APP receipt handshake
or lose the result. There is no unconditional receipt on grant and no retry.
This lossy one-result transport is an explicit new abstraction. Cancellation
or deadline disables delivery and retires remaining job/measurement work.

APP Completed requires active admitted matching request/job/sample/transport
identities, produced/enqueued/dispatched/attempted sample, no prior receipt
or failure/cancel, consistent stored detection quality, and all strict UAV
requirements. The class predicates retain their historical meaning: strict
requires OK, normal disallows FAILED, relaxed allows any class, with the
historical consistency exclusions and hard SLA failures retained. Freshness
and update bands are computed from the actual matching sample age at receipt:
[0,5) FRESH/UPD_OK; [5,10) STALE/UPD_SLOW; >=10 EXPIRED/UPD_VIOLATED.
Thus age 5 is already stale and age 10 expired. Strict UAV needs age <5.
The historical mapping's update class is an age-band abstraction, not an
empirical inter-sample period. Request age <=40 includes admission; age 40
allows receipt or timeout nondeterministically, and age >40 cannot succeed.

Receipt records immutable IDs, quality/timeliness flags and service age band
(1: <40; 2: =40), sample band (0: <5) on the actual delivery edge. Exact real
ages are retained in the receipt state's engine DBM; standard UPPAAL clocks
cannot be copied into integer payloads. Terminal clocks keep advancing but
cannot revoke an earlier success. Queries use these retrospective records.

Rejected is admission failure (outcome 2); ServiceFailed is sensing/queue/
transmission/invalid-result failure (3); ServiceTimeout is deadline failure
(4); Cancelled is safety termination (5). None sets success. The old absolute
time-40 completion event and termination-to-completion synchronization are
removed. Safety termination still follows the original SLA policy and cancels
an active request before any result guard can accept it. Later reports after
success do not retroactively change the completion record.

Accepted locations have service_age<=40 and an unsynchronized timeout at
equality; JobMeasuring has measurement_age<=5 with success/failure or inactive
release; Transmitting has tx_age<=1 with delivery/loss or inactive release.
These added bounds have failure exits. No new committed or urgent location
is introduced. Historical committed staging and existing unrelated deadlines
remain; global deadlock freedom is an open query, not a structural guarantee.

Changes: global declarations; u0_app_A_REQ, u0_phy_Template_A_SQ,
u0_Boundary_E_SERVICE and SharedLoad; added C82_ResultJob/c82_job. All original
20 core, 22 observer and 8 boundary instances remain in system order, plus
the job last (51 total). changes.json enumerates all locations/edges/guards/
assignments/synchronizations in the changed templates; inventory.json lists
clocks, channels, queries and hashes. Other templates are structurally exact.
The production generator, old evidence, manifests and manuscript stay outside
this candidate's write scope. Review and a new baseline decision are required
before downstream verification claims or integration.
